### Signed numbers

A trailing-overpunch field is all digits, with the sign carried in the zone of the last byte:

| last byte | digit | sign |
|---|---|---|
| `0`-`9` | same digit | + |
| `{`, `A`-`I` | 0, 1-9 | + |
| `}`, `J`-`R` | 0, 1-9 | - |
| `p`-`y` | 0-9 | - |

GnuCOBOL writes the letter forms with `-fsign=EBCDIC` (this answer key was built with it); write what the answer key's files hold.

## How the weigh judges it

Tare decodes your files and the answer key's with the layouts above. Records are matched by key; every declared field is compared, numbers in decimal. The weigh is balanced only when every field of every record is equal and no record is missing or extra. `tare.json` lists differences a person has accepted (under `accepted`, signed with `python -m tare accept`); an accepted difference does not turn the weigh red, and every weigh still shows it. The gate hashes `port/**/*.java` and `port/MAIN` at each weigh; any later edit needs a new weigh before a commit.
